Maintenance Guidelines

Follow these simple steps to report and manage college maintenance issues correctly.

Before Reporting an Issue

Please check the following points before submitting a maintenance request:

  • Report genuine maintenance problems that require attention.
  • Check whether the same issue has already been reported.
  • Make sure you enter the correct room number, building, floor, or location.
  • Choose the correct maintenance category such as Electrical, Plumbing, Furniture, Cleaning, or Other.

How to Submit a Request

  • Give a short and clear title for the problem.
  • Describe what happened and where the problem is located.
  • Mention important details such as a damaged item, water leakage, broken light, or faulty equipment.
  • Upload a clear photo when it helps the maintenance team understand the problem.
  • Review your information before clicking Submit Request.

After Submitting a Request

  • Save your Request ID for future reference.
  • Use My Requests to view requests submitted by you.
  • Use Track Request to check the latest status.
  • Do not submit multiple requests for the same problem unless the original request needs additional information.
  • If the problem is urgent or creates a safety risk, inform the concerned college staff in addition to submitting the online request.

Quick Reminder

Be clear, be accurate, and provide enough information. A well-described request helps the maintenance team understand the problem and take appropriate action more easily.
